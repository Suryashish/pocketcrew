How it works

From sign-up to first reply
in under a minute.

  1. 01

    Describe an agent

    Type one sentence, like a patient maths tutor for my ten-year-old. Gemma writes its name, role and instructions.

  2. 02

    Tap to connect

    One button, or one QR code. Your Telegram chat is linked to your crew. You never touch BotFather.

  3. 03

    Just type

    Send a message. The dispatcher picks the agent, and the answer lands in the same chat.
